# Minemist Rules
Play fair, be nice
Remember Misty is always watching.

# 1. Show respect
- No discrimination, hate speech, or slurs.
- No impersonating other players or staff.
- No releasing personal or private information (doxing).

# 2. Keep chat usable
- Keep it clean (no spam, excessive caps, NSFW content, or advertising).
- Stick to English in global chats.
- Keep real world political/controversial conversations away from global chats.

# 3. Play fairly
- No hacking, xray, fast-place, auto-clicking, or exploits of any kind.
- No diminishing server performance (lag machines, bots, chunk loaders, etc).
- Raiding in the **wilderness** is permitted provided it follows the [**Raiding Rules**](/Rules#raiding-rules).
- No stealing or griefing of any kind inside towns.

# 4. Be mature
- No toxic behaviour (don’t try to ruin someone else’s experience).
- Don’t engage in arguments.
- PvP is only allowed in a war, pvp arena or if a player agrees to fight.
- Be civil in global chat (No “edgy humour”, slandering, or disrespectful remarks).

# 5. Build responsibly
- New towns & outposts must be created at least 25 chunks away from already established towns.
- Builds & claims must be fair (no claim encasing, town spawn traps, etc).
- No block spam, lava casts, obscene, or overly laggy builds (i.e. redstone, entities & open hoppers).
- No part or whole destruction/griefing anywhere.

# 6. Keep the economy fair
- Trading, buying and selling must not involve real world value transactions.
- No using towns, nations, or alts for unfair resource/tax advantages.
- No selling privileged items (i.e. /skulls, non-dye coloured sign text, non-anvil named items)
- No scams, gambling or unfair trades.

# 7. Be responsible for your actions
- You're responsible for your account(s) and actions they take.
- Alts are allowed for time convenience only (i.e. AFKing at a farm. Not for giving an unfair advantage or to bypass a ban/mute).
- Staff decisions are final ([appeal the decision](<https://minemist.com/appeal>) if you believe otherwise).


# Raiding Rules
For the most part breaking into and raiding unclaimed player builds is allowed with a few exceptions to keep gameplay fair. We are not a griefing server and do not allow total destruction of a build anywhere.
**By raiding we mean**: players may interact with unclaimed builds to steal resources from unlocked chests or mob/crop farms, but must do so in a way that doesn't damage the build significantly.

The Raiding Rules change depending on if it’s inside a town or in the wilderness.

### Everywhere

- Any low effort (lava casts, block towers) or obscene builds will be removed by staff regardless of location.
- Towns are not permitted to be created for the purpose of claiming over someone else's build, or circumventing raiding rules (i.e. claiming within 25 chunks of someone’s build so you can remove it).
- Total destruction or pointless damage is **not** permitted.

### In Wilderness

*The wilderness is a contested zone that is not owned by a town or nation. Be careful when building within the wilderness as it may be raided.*

- Any build located in the wilderness is vulnerable to raiding.
- Any wilderness plots that are 2 chunks away from a town plot are classified as inside town claims for raiding rules and follows the **In Towns** rules below.
- Items that are not in locked chests are permitted to be taken.
- Buildings may be broken into to get access to the inside.
- Mobs may be killed and farms (including grinder type farms) may be looted.
- Building/breaking close to the border of a town (within 25 chunks) without permission from the town mayor allows the town mayor (or delegate) to remove the whole build or fix the terrain without returning items, provided their town was created before your build.
- After a war, any blocks placed/broken outside the town must be reset by the town members who placed them upon request of the attacked town (these items do not need to be returned). 

### In Towns

- Towns that are in ruins are **not** permitted to be raided until the reclaim period has expired.
- Chests (locked and unlocked) located in ruined towns are **not** permitted to be raided until the reclaim period has expired.
- Mobs within a ruined town are permitted to be killed even if the reclaim period has not expired.
- Farms within a ruined town (including grinder type farms) are permitted to be looted even if the reclaim period has not expired.
- All builds/mobs/chests inside town claims are protected from any destruction/raiding/stealing and must not be modified without permission from the build creator or town mayor (or delegate).
- Builds within a town created by a resident are permitted to be removed by a town mayor (or delegate), provided all the items/blocks are securely stored for 30 days minimum with free access for that resident, and an attempt to contact the resident (using /mail or the likes) to collect the items/blocks has been made.
- Items/blocks that have not been collected by a resident after the 30 day minimum has lapsed can either continue to be stored or repossessed by the town mayor (or delegate), provided this process is clearly stated in your town rules.
