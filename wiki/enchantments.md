# Enchantments
Minemist changes how enchantments are traditionally obtained, instead of relying on villagers to get enchantments you can however obtain tablets when mining ores and use them to craft enchantments. There are other methods to obtain enchantments including the enchantment table (no treasure enchants), treasure loot e.g. fishing, chests and mob loot. 

You can also obtain custom enchantments from the plugin [ExcellentEnchants](https://www.spigotmc.org/resources/excellentenchants-%E2%AD%90-80-vanilla-like-enchantments.61693/).
