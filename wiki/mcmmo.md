# Misc Skills
## Acrobatics (Maxes 1000)
Acrobatics skill grants a chance to negate fall damage (Roll/Graceful) and a chance to halve incoming attack damage (Dodge).

XP is gained from taking fall damage (or damage in general for some reason) with the XP being doubled if you have Feather Falling on your boots (can be any level).

Roll gives a chance to negate fall damage when landing negating a max of 7HP or 14 HP if sneaking (Graceful roll). The chance is 100% at max level, graceful roll has doubled chance.
Dodge has a max 20% chance at level 1000 to halve incoming damage, this doesn’t proc if the halved damage is still lethal. 

## Alchemy (Maxes 1000)
Alchemy skill speeds up the brewing process (Catalysis) and unlocks access to brewing potions not available in vanilla Minecraft (Concoctions).

XP is gained by brewing potions and more XP is gained when applying modifiers like gunpowder.

Catalysis increases the speed of all brewing options to a max of 4x brewing speed at level 1000.
Concoctions unlock additional brewing recipes, each recipe is tied to the skill rank level with all recipes unlocked at level 1000. These recipes include Haste (Carrot), Dullness (Slimeball), Absorption (Quartz), Health Boost (Apple), Hunger (Rotten Flesh), Nausea (Brown Mushroom), Blindness (Ink Sac), Saturation (Fern), Decay (Poisonous Potato), Resistance (Golden Apple).

## Reapir (Maxes 1000)
Repair skill allows you to restore the durability of tools and armours using the mcMMO anvil. Your Repair skill level determines the durability that is restored (Repair Mastery), whether that durability being restored will be doubled (Super Repair) and chance to keep enchantments (Arcane Forging).

XP is gained by repairing items with their respective materials e.g. Iron Helmet with Iron Ingots. The better the material is used, the more XP is gained.

To use the mcMMO anvil (iron block), place it on the ground and hold the item you want to repair and right click twice to consume the material required and restore durability.

Repair Mastery increases the durability restored on every repair to a cap of 200% bonus durability restored resulting in every.
Super Repair gives a percentage chance to double the amount repaired on each use maxing out at 100% chance to activate, this is applied after the Repair Mastery bonus.
For every enchant on an item Arcane Forging will make two rolls, one for a percent chance to keep the enchant otherwise it is deleted, and another for if it is keeping the enchant whether it should downgrade the level of the enchant.

## Salvage (Maxes 1000)
Salvage skill lets you break down tools and armour at a Salvage Anvil to recover base materials and potentially extract enchantments as an enchanted book. Higher ranks (Scrap Collector) recovers more materials and you can increase your chance to preserve enchantments (Arcane Salvage).

The level of your Salvage Skill is determined by the total level of your Repair and Fishing skill halved e.g. Repair 1000 + Fishing 1000 = Salvage 1000

To use the mcMMO salvage anvil (gold block), place it on the ground and hold the item you want to salvage and right click twice to consume the item to drop its materials and potentially an enchanted book with the items enchants.

Scrap Collector determines the maximum materials recovered when salvaging up to 16. The items returned are both affected by the rank of Scrap Collector and the durability of the item.
Arcane Salvage gives a chance to extract enchantments from a salvaged item and produce an enchanted book. For each enchantment it is rolled to check whether it is fully extracted or partially extracted.
