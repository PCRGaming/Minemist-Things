# How to create a shrine
> To create a shrine you have to be the mayor of a town.
{.is-info}
1. Within the designated chunk you want your shrine to be in, set the plot to be a shrine plot by using `/plot set shrine`.
2. Place a netherite block, a lodestone and then a sign on the netherite block. 
3. On the sign either write `[Balance]`, `[Power]` or `[Freedom]`. Currently choosing a specific culture doesn’t do anything so feel free to choose any.
4. To complete the shrine place an end rod on top of the lodestone.

![create-shrine.png](/create-shrine.png)

# What to use a shrine for
Shrines are used to upgrade normal tablets from level 1-3 to level 4 or 5 and make shrine tablets which are used to craft useful enchantments like mending.

## How to obtain tablets
Tablets level 1-3 are dropped when you mine ores at a chance of 1-2%, increased by 0.2% per fortune level, the type of tablet dropped depends on what job you chose at the start of the game (Balance, Power, Freedom).
To make a tablet level 4 or 5, combine tablets level 1-3 with a slime ball and amethyst shard in the GUI.
![higher-enchants.png](/higher-enchants.png)

> If you want to combine level 1 or 2 tablets to make a level 2 or 3 use a crafting table with a slime ball and amethyst shard instead. E.g. level 1 + level 2 = level 3 tablet.
{.is-warning}

## How to make a shrine tablet
To make a shrine tablet combine 1 of each type of tablet with a level of 1-3 so that they add up to a value of **<u>6-8</u>** e.g. Balance 1 + Power 2 + Freedom 3 with a slimeball and amethyst shard.

# What are tablets used for
See the [Enchantments page](/Enchantments) for how you can use those tablets, a book and the material list in a <u>**Crafting Table**</u> to create a book with the enchantment at a level equal to the tablet level. If an enchantment crafted is a level over the max vanilla level when combining in an anvil it will revert back to the vanilla cap.
