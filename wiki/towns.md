# The Beginnings
`/t create <town-name>` - Command to create a new town on the chunk you are currently standing in. It will cost 2000❖. If an error occurs check that you are not in an already claimed chunk e.g.
![claimed-chunk.png](/claimed-chunk.png)
If the chunk you want to claim is within 25 chunks of another town, you would not be able to start your town or claim a chunk there. 
![chunk-too-close.png](/chunk-too-close.png)

## Basic Town Commands
`/t <town-name>` - Shows basic information about a town like their balance and members.

`/t add <player>` - Sends an invite to player to join your town, will fail if they are already in a town.

`accept <town-name>` - Accepts an town invite.

---

## Claiming Land
`/t claim` - Claims the chunk you are standing on costing 200❖, this will only work the chunk is connected to an already claimed chunk. Some other methods of claiming:

* `/t claim rect [1-4]` - claims a square with radius you provided
* `/t claim circle [1-5]` - claims a circle with radius you provided
* `/t claim fill` - claims all chunks within a border of your claims
* `/t claim auto` - 
* `/t claim outpost` - you can claim chunks not connected to your town for a higher cost of 1500❖ but it has to not be near another town.

* `\t unclaim` - returns a claimed chunk to the wilderness refunding 50❖ per chunk.
